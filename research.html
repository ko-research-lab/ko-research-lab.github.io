Research

Research Overview

The Ko Research Lab develops computational and AI methods for intelligent infrastructure systems. A unifying thread across our work is uncertainty — how to represent it, quantify it, and act on it when modeling and managing structural and infrastructure systems. Our research sits at the intersection of infrastructure, AI, and uncertainty.

Uncertainty-Aware Artificial Intelligence

Probabilistic ML · Bayesian deep learning · Trustworthy AI

We build AI and machine-learning methods that explicitly represent and quantify uncertainty. Rather than producing a single point prediction, our models communicate how confident they are, enabling engineers to make risk-informed decisions about infrastructure.

Structural and Infrastructure Systems

Structural engineering · Civil infrastructure · Life-cycle management

Our methods are grounded in the physics and engineering of real structural and infrastructure systems — bridges, buildings, and civil networks — so that computational tools translate into practical improvements in safety, performance, and resilience.

Uncertainty Quantification and Reliability

UQ · Reliability analysis · Risk assessment

We develop uncertainty quantification and reliability methods that propagate uncertainty from data and models through to engineering predictions, supporting reliability analysis and risk-informed decision-making.

State Estimation and Bayesian Filtering

Kalman & particle filters · Data assimilation · Dynamic systems

Using Bayesian filtering and state-estimation techniques, we infer the hidden states of dynamic systems from noisy sensor data, combining physical models with measurements to track how systems evolve over time.

System Identification

Parameter estimation · Model calibration · Inverse problems

We estimate the parameters and governing models of physical systems directly from observed data, calibrating models so that they faithfully reflect the behavior of the real structures they represent.

Structural Health Monitoring

SHM · Damage detection · Sensing & diagnostics

We advance structural health monitoring methods that detect, localize, and characterize damage using sensor data, helping to keep aging infrastructure safe and informing maintenance decisions.

Digital Twins

Virtual replicas · Real-time modeling · Predictive management

We build digital twins — living, data-driven digital representations of physical infrastructure — that stay synchronized with their real counterparts to support prediction, diagnosis, and management throughout a system's life cycle.

Physics-Informed and Scientific Machine Learning

PINNs · SciML · Physics-constrained learning

By embedding physical laws directly into learning algorithms, we develop physics-informed and scientific machine-learning methods that respect known physics, generalize from limited data, and remain interpretable for engineering use.
