Ko Research Lab

AI, Uncertainty, and Intelligent Infrastructure Systems

We develop computational and artificial-intelligence methods for intelligent infrastructure systems — working at the intersection of infrastructure, AI, and uncertainty. Our research aims to make the modeling, monitoring, and management of structural and infrastructure systems more reliable, data-driven, and uncertainty-aware.

Principal Investigator: Minhyeok Ko
Department of Civil & Construction Engineering and Management
The University of Texas at Tyler

Join the Lab

Research

Three core themes

Our work spans uncertainty-aware machine learning, infrastructure systems engineering, and digital representations of the physical world.

Uncertainty-Aware AI

AI and machine-learning methods that explicitly represent and quantify uncertainty, so that predictions come with trustworthy confidence rather than a single number.

Intelligent Infrastructure Systems

Data-driven and physics-informed methods for monitoring, modeling, and managing structural and infrastructure systems throughout their life cycle.

Digital Twins & System Identification

State estimation, parameter estimation, Bayesian filtering, structural health monitoring, and digital representations of physical infrastructure.
