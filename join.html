Join Us

Join the Lab

We welcome curious, motivated researchers interested in AI, uncertainty, and intelligent infrastructure systems. Backgrounds in civil/structural engineering, computer science, applied mathematics, statistics, or related fields are all a good fit.

We are currently recruiting M.S. and undergraduate researchers.

Prospective Master's (M.S.) Students

We are currently recruiting M.S. students who are excited to work on uncertainty-aware AI and its application to structural and infrastructure systems. Strong programming skills and a solid foundation in mathematics or engineering mechanics are valuable.

How to apply: Email mko@uttyler.edu with the subject line “Prospective MS Students - Ko Research Lab” and attach:
  • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP)
  • Transcript (unofficial acceptable)

Undergraduate Researchers

Undergraduate students at UT Tyler interested in research experience are encouraged to reach out. Opportunities may include independent study, senior projects, and paid or credit-based research positions.

How to get involved: Email mko@uttyler.edu with your transcript (unofficial acceptable) and a brief note about your interests.
