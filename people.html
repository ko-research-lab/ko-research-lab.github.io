Principal Investigator

Minhyeok Ko

Principal Investigator
Assistant Professor (Tenure-Track), Civil & Construction Engineering and Management
The University of Texas at Tyler

Research interests: physics-informed digital twins for civil infrastructure (with an emphasis on wind turbines and renewable energy systems); structural health monitoring and real-time state estimation; uncertainty quantification, reliability, and fragility analysis; data assimilation and Bayesian filtering (Kalman and particle filters); and physics-informed / scientific machine learning.

Education

  • Ph.D., Civil Engineering (Structural Engineering) — The Pennsylvania State University, 2023
  • M.S., Civil Engineering (Structural Engineering) — Northwestern University, 2016
  • M.E., Structural Engineering in Architecture — Chung-Ang University, 2014
  • B.S., Architectural Engineering (Structure) — Chung-Ang University, 2012
